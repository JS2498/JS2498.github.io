I'm an **AI/ML Research Specialist** at **Nokia Standards**, currently working on LLM-based retrieval workflows, RAG pipelines, and deep learning models for 6G physical layer research.

I hold an **MS (Research)** degree from the **Indian Institute of Technology, Dharwad**, where I worked on the **Optimization of Age of Information in Wireless Communication Networks** under [Asst. Prof. Rajshekhar V Bhat](https://sites.google.com/view/rajshekhar-v-bhat/home). My research interests span Machine Learning, Reinforcement Learning, Stochastic Optimization, and applied AI systems.
